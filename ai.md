[artificial intelligence][ai]
Artificial intelligence enables computer systems to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and decision-making.

The system goes through three steps repeatedly: First, it attempts the task (makes a prediction). Second, it compares its output to the correct answer and calculates the error. Third, it adjusts its internal parameters to reduce that error.
This happens thousands of times across many examples, gradually improving the system's ability to recognize the right patterns.

AI fundamentally works through pattern recognition.. [machine learning]
    • During Training: The system recognizes and learns patterns from examples.
    • During Operation: The trained system receives new cases and compares them against the patterns it learned during training to make decisions.
